Rock Mail

A fast, private mail app for your own mail server, on iPhone and Mac.

Support Privacy Policy

What it does

  • JMAP first, IMAP too. Sign in to a JMAP server (such as Stalwart or Fastmail) for instant updates, or to any IMAP + SMTP account.
  • Conversations grouped like Mail, with search that works offline.
  • Rules that run on your server, so mail is sorted even when your devices are off (JMAP servers with Sieve).
  • Privacy built in. Remote images are held back unless you allow a sender, and tracking pixels are removed.
  • Works offline. Read, reply, file and delete without a connection; changes go out when you're back online, with Undo Send.
  • Notification briefs written on your device by Apple Intelligence, where available.

No account with us

Rock Mail talks directly to your mail server. There's no Rock Mail account, no tracking and no analytics. See the privacy policy.
